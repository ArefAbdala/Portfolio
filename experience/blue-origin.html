Pneumatic Operations Jan 2025 – Feb 2026

Role Overview

Full-time Pneumatic Operations team member at Blue Origin's Launch Complex 36 (LC-36) in Cape Canaveral, supporting ground and vehicle pneumatic systems during active launch campaigns. Working at the intersection of ground support equipment and vehicle operations for next-generation lunar landing systems.

Key Responsibilities

  • Control room operator for ground and vehicle pneumatic systems at LC-36
  • Developed MK1 lunar lander cryogenic & pneumatic loading CONOPS through launch campaign
  • Implemented and executed corrective and maintenance programs from ground systems
  • Coordinated pneumatic system operations with launch campaign milestones and schedules

Technical Environment

Operations at LC-36 involve complex pneumatic and cryogenic systems supporting the New Glenn vehicle and MK1 lunar lander development program. The role requires deep understanding of high-pressure gas systems, cryogenic fluid handling, and launch operations protocols in a safety-critical environment.

Programs Supported

  • New Glenn — LC-36 ground systems and infrastructure
  • MK1 Lunar Lander — cryogenic & pneumatic loading CONOPS development
  • Ground Systems Maintenance — corrective and preventive maintenance programs
